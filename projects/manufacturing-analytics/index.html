From local measurements to global comparisons

A global automotive manufacturer wanted to understand overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) across its shop floors. The practical need was to compare performance between shifts and locations, then use those comparisons to guide improvement.

I built a global OEE calculation and analytics solution that enabled shift-to-shift and shop-floor-to-shop-floor comparisons. The work helped the customer improve productivity and reduce manufacturing cycle times.

This is the kind of customer problem I work on at AWS: translate an operational question into a data and cloud architecture that engineering and business teams can use.

My contribution

I connected the manufacturing requirements with solution architecture and implementation. The OEE solution made cross-shift and cross-floor comparisons available for operational analysis, giving the customer a way to examine performance beyond a single local view.

The broader work required customer-facing technical leadership: architecture discussions, workload strategy, and collaboration with sales, business development, and technical teams. I create executive-ready solution guidance and field enablement to connect technical decisions with adoption.

Industrial IoT and machine learning

My AWS manufacturing work spans industrial IoT, machine learning, computer vision, and time-series analytics. I design architectures for industrial data ingestion, streaming analytics, and edge-to-cloud integration, with security, governance, scale, and observability as part of the design.

Those disciplines connect physical operations with the cloud: how data is collected, how it is organized over time, where analysis runs, and how results reach the people using them. My role is to connect that technical work to the customer’s operational requirements.

AWS and data engineering breadth

I bring broad familiarity across the AWS ML and data analytics stack, supported by hands-on customer architecture and engineering work. The toolbox spans several connected areas:

  • ML and generative AI: Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Bedrock, and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.
  • Industrial and streaming data: AWS IoT, AWS IoT Greengrass, Amazon Kinesis, and Kafka.
  • Data platforms and analytics: Amazon S3, AWS Glue, Amazon EMR, Amazon Redshift, Amazon RDS/Aurora, Amazon OpenSearch Service, and Amazon QuickSight.
  • Deployment and operations: Amazon EKS, AWS Lambda, AWS Step Functions, CloudFormation, CloudWatch, container orchestration, and CI/CD.

My earlier work at Informatica and Wipro adds depth in data quality, governance, MDM, ETL/ELT, migration, and operational recovery. That foundation is useful when AI and analytics depend on trustworthy data and repeatable operations.

Technical leadership and adoption

With 20+ years of experience, I combine implementation with executive advisory, mentoring, technical roadmap ownership, and organization-wide adoption. I work across the transition from customer requirements to an implementable architecture and an operational solution.

The OEE project illustrates that approach: a concrete manufacturing need, a global analytics capability, and comparisons that support operational decisions.
