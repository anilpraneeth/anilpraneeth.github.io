Manufacturing platform · In development

Quatrion

Spatial tokenization · CAD · FEA · CFD · Forming

Quatrion on GitHub ↗

Y Combinator application submitted.

I’m building an agentic platform for manufacturing companies, connecting natural-language requests to CAD and simulation services.

  • Spatial tokenization. Encodes 3D positions and orientations, assembly hierarchy, and part relationships into structured context that bridges LLMs and CAD, with bounded views and source provenance.
  • Custom FEA solvers. FEniCSx and PETSc solvers for linear and nonlinear structural analysis, including large deformation, J2 plasticity, GPU-accelerated sparse solves, and analytical benchmark tests.
  • CFD and forming workflows. CFD case validation and postprocessing, plus sheet-metal forming through OpenRadioss integration, asynchronous jobs, and solver-output KPI extraction.
  • Agent and deployment workflows. Structured tool calling, configurable model providers, reusable Codex and Claude Code skills, and Kubernetes and Helm deployment automation for cloud and on-premises targets.
